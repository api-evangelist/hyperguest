# HyperGuest

> HyperGuest is a B2B hospitality distribution platform that connects hotels directly with travel demand partners through a single unified connectivity API, removing wholesaler and intermediary layers so accommodation suppliers and travel providers can transact real-time availability, rates, and bookings at 100% direct rates. It integrates with 100+ PMS, channel-manager, and travel-tech systems and offers an adaptive distribution engine (Revenue Compass) plus direct payment settlement.

## APIs

- [HyperGuest Connectivity API](https://www.hyperguest.com/travel-providers): Unified connectivity API for real-time availability, rates, and bookings. Base URL https://api.hyperguest.com. Access is partner-provisioned and authentication-gated; no public OpenAPI is published.

## Solutions

- [For Hotels](https://www.hyperguest.com/hotels): Direct distribution to travel providers without wholesaler layers, with pricing/inventory control and direct payment settlement.
- [For Travel Providers](https://www.hyperguest.com/travel-providers): Single API integration to a global hotel network at 100% direct rates.
- [Integrations](https://www.hyperguest.com/integrations): 100+ live PMS, channel-manager, and travel-tech integrations (Mews, Cloudbeds, SiteMinder, Oracle Opera Cloud, Shiji, D-Edge, TravelgateX, and more).

## Security & Compliance

- [Security & Vulnerability Disclosure](https://www.hyperguest.com/security): Responsible-disclosure policy with safe harbor, 90-day disclosure window, security@hyperguest.com. No bug bounty.
- [PCI DSS Compliance](https://www.hyperguest.com/pci-dss-compliance): Annual PCI DSS audit by QSA Adsigo with Attestation of Compliance.
- [GDPR](https://www.hyperguest.com/gdpr): GDPR program with Data Protection Officer (gdpr@hyperguest.com).
- [PSD2](https://www.hyperguest.com/psd2): PSD2 compliance for payment handling.
